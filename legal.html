Terms of Service

Who we are

"Ask Amy" is an AI receptionist service operated by Askamy LLC ("we," "us"). Amy answers inbound telephone calls placed to our customers' business phone lines, qualifies the caller, and books appointments into the customer's scheduling calendar.

Who our customers are

Our service is sold to businesses (B2B) — home service companies such as chimney, HVAC, and plumbing contractors. By subscribing, you confirm you are purchasing for a business, not as a consumer.

The service

Your subscription provides access to an AI receptionist configured for your business. The service answers the inbound calls your customers place to your business line. We do not place outbound marketing or sales calls as part of this service.

Billing

Plans are billed as a recurring monthly subscription, in advance, to the payment method on file. Each plan includes a set allotment of answered-call volume as described on our pricing page; usage beyond the included allotment may be billed as an overage in arrears. A one-time build fee applies at signup as listed on the pricing page.

Your responsibilities

  • Maintain control of your own phone number and call forwarding settings.
  • Use the service only for lawful business purposes.

Refunds & Cancellation

Cancel anytime

Subscriptions are month to month after the initial build. Cancel by emailing hello@askamycall.com from the email on your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — Amy keeps answering until then, and you will not be charged again.

Refunds

  • Monthly subscription fees are not refunded once a billing period has started, since the service is live and answering for the full period.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • From our customers (the businesses): account and contact details, business configuration (pricing, service area, scheduling rules), and payment information. Payments are processed by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • From callers to our customers' phone lines: when Amy answers a call, the call may be recorded and transcribed to book the appointment and to deliver the lead to the business the caller dialed. This can include the caller's name, phone number, address, and details of the requested service.

How we use it

  • To provide the service — answering calls, booking appointments, and delivering lead details to the business the caller contacted.
  • To improve call handling quality for that business.
  • To bill our customers and operate our business.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell caller data or customer data to third parties.
  • We do not use caller information to contact callers for marketing.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the service — including telephony/AI voice infrastructure, cloud hosting and databases, scheduling integrations, and payment processing. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Retention

Call records and transcripts are retained as long as needed to provide the service to the business and for reasonable business record-keeping, after which they may be deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

Businesses may request deletion of their account data by emailing hello@askamycall.com. Callers who wish to have their information removed may contact the business they called or email us and we will assist.
